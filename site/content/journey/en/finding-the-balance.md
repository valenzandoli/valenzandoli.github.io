A few months into training with running and lifting in the same week, here's what I've learned so far:

- **Sequencing matters more than I expected.** Running the day after a heavy lower-body session was consistently bad — adjusted to put easy runs the day after, and harder runs further away from leg day.
- **Volume creeps up quietly.** It's easy to add "just one more set" or "just a bit further" each week without noticing the total load until recovery suffers.
- **Tracking helps catch this early.** A simple weekly log of sessions and how they felt made the pattern obvious in a way that "just remembering" never did.

Next focus: holding training volume steady for a few weeks and seeing how recovery responds before adding anything else.
