I've trained on and off for years, but never kept a real record of it. Starting this log for a simple reason: the same tracking mindset that helps with operations data helps with training too — and writing it down in public adds a bit of accountability.

**Where I'm at right now:**

- Training split: hybrid — strength/hypertrophy work 3-4x/week, plus running and mobility work.
- Focus: building a consistent base before pushing intensity, especially on the running side.
- Goal for the next few months: get comfortable running longer distances without it eating into recovery for lifting.

No dramatic before/after photos for now — just a starting point to look back on later.
